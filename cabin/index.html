    Wood stove & gas fireplace

    Wood stove (the red one)

    Damper handleLeft side, toward the back. Back is open, down is closed. No in-between.
    Air leverRight side. Pull it forward for more heat.
    Door handleThe removable handle lives in a holder by the front leg.
    Front doorsSlide the handle onto the door latch and turn it clockwise to open.
    Ash doorKeep it latched while there's a fire.
    1. Light it. Damper open, air all the way forward. Newspaper, kindling and a few small splits, then light it. For a faster start, crack the front door and stay with it until the kindling catches, then close it.
    2. Build it up. Add bigger wood as it catches. Once it's roaring and there's a bed of glowing coals, close the damper.
    3. Set the heat with the air lever. Keep the dial on the stovepipe in its middle zone.
    4. To add wood, open the damper, then open the door slowly. Load it, shut the door, close the damper.

    Dry wood only. Keep anything that can burn a few feet back. This baby gets HOT.

    The lake chain

    Pokegama is one of ten connected, spring-fed lakes in the Lac du Flambeau chain, about 8,000 acres of water in all.

    Fence Lake is the biggest, with musky, walleye, northern, smallmouth and panfish. Long Interlaken runs along downtown Lac du Flambeau. Lake of the Torches casino sits on Pokegama's shore.

    Fishing

    In Pokegama

    • Walleye
    • Musky
    • Northern pike
    • Largemouth and smallmouth bass
    • Panfish

    1,041 acres, 65 feet at the deepest, and very clear. The bottom is mostly sand, with rock and gravel.

    Depth map

    Darker water is deeper, and the lines are depths in feet. The red dot is the cabin. Traced from onX Fish, so treat it as approximate and not for navigation. Tap to enlarge.

    Where to start

    • Walleye and musky: points, bars and the edges where the shallows drop to the basin.
    • Musky: fallen timber along the shoreline, especially with topwater.
    • Panfish: standing weeds offshore, with small jigs.

    Licenses and rules

    Pokegama is inside the Lac du Flambeau reservation, and the tribe requires non-members to carry a Lac du Flambeau fishing license. Pick one up at Lac du Flambeau Campground & Marina, the same place we recommend for pontoons. Follow Wisconsin's seasons and bag limits.

    Pokegama is one of 19 reservation lakes where the Lac du Flambeau Band voted in April 2026 to stop non-members from keeping walleye and musky. A federal court has blocked that rule while the case goes on, so for now the state rules apply. The Band asks anglers not to keep or target walleye and musky. Check for updates before keeping either one.

    Some boat landings on tribal land require a launch permit for non-members.
